## Theoretical boundaries to preserve

- EAP and academic ELF are presented as different starting orientations, not mutually exclusive fields.
- Mauranen, Hynninen, and Ranta's writing-versus-speech contrast is historical; ELF also includes written communication.
- The claim about Anglo-American textbooks is bounded to the reported Chinese EMI context. Do not generalise it to every EMI programme or publisher.
- Written textbook prose can be treated as written linguistic input. MIWAE extends input modification to disciplinary communication and must not equate modification with simplification alone.
- McKinley concerns academic-literacy preparedness rather than a textbook scale. MIWAE's textbook connection is an explicit inference.
- CEFR describes user/learner competence. Its mapping to textbook design is MIWAE's operational extension.
- MIWAE remains a provisional framework and review procedure, not a validated universal scale.
